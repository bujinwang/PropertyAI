### Positioning notes, product by product

**TenantCloud — the value king of the spreadsheet-replacement tier, now coasting on a dead free plan.** Its entire identity was "free up to 75 units"; that plan was removed in the 2021 repricing, yet the free story still does its marketing. The clever bit of its current model is **unlimited units, capped leases (10/30/60)** — it prices *activity*, not *doors*, which is why per-lease cost falls to $1.00–$1.80 at cap and looks unbeatable. It is the only one of the four with genuinely deep self-manager accounting plus a real owner portal, and it is the closest thing here to a **T2-capable product at T1 prices** — but it stops exactly where a small PM firm starts: **no trust accounting, no bank reconciliation below Pro, no SMS, payment holds, and AI-first support.** *Inference: TenantCloud is PropertyAI's most likely **displacement** target in the 10–60-lease band, because its users are already paying and already hitting its lease caps.*

**Hemlane — the only one selling humans, not just software.** Where the other three sell a tool, Hemlane sells a **ladder of delegation**: free software → $2/unit software → $20/unit *a repair coordinator* → $68/unit *a management assistant*. Its ICP is the **remote/out-of-state owner** who wants more than DIY but refuses a 8–10% management fee. That is a defensible, hard-to-copy position (it requires a service organisation, not code), and the free-forever tier is the real acquisition engine. The costs are structural: per-unit pricing on the service tiers soon exceeds an actual PM fee, the free tier withholds rent collection, and there is **no trust accounting**. *Inference: Hemlane is a **services** competitor, not a software one — PropertyAI cannot beat "someone else takes the 2 AM call" with a feature list; it can only beat it on price-per-door and on AI-first coverage without a human coordinator.*

**Rentec Direct — the transparency purist, and the most credible T2 wedge of the four.** Rentec is what the category looked like before consolidation: founder-owned, self-funded, **prices printed in public on a live slider with no email gate**, no minimums, no contracts, a free trial **with your own data** whose work survives conversion, and free onboarding at any unit count. It is the only one of the four that ships **trust accounting** *and* **1099 e-filing** *and* a **free public Open API** — i.e. the three things a small PM firm actually needs to run someone else's money. Its weakness is the flip side of its maturity: **dated UX, no manager mobile app, no AI beyond listing copy, and a small review footprint.** *Inference: Rentec PM at $65/mo is the **real head-to-head** for PropertyAI's Growth tier — PropertyAI must beat it on AI depth and UX, and must at minimum match trust accounting, 1099 e-filing and a published price.*

**MagicDoor — the same story as PropertyAI, told from below, at a tenth of the entry price.** Where the other three bolted AI onto features people already bought (a listing writer, a "line item"), MagicDoor **is** the AI-native platform: Genie runs maintenance triage, screening scores, comms, renewals, and accounting, and it is **included on every plan with no AI add-on gate**. Its pricing is the sharpest weapon in this document: **$2.50 per *active lease* per month** — which means (a) a 1-unit landlord pays $2.50 (or $20 for up to 10 leases), (b) **no minimum, no setup fee, no contract, no credit card to start**, (c) it bills *occupancy*, not doors, so vacancy months are free. It also does the things a T2 buyer needs — **trust accounting, owner statements, 1099s via IRS IRIS, an in-house e-signature with no per-envelope fee, and Section 8 HAP modeling** — with a **done-for-you migration in 3–7 days**. Its only real defences against it are **immaturity (founded 2023, near-zero review corpus) and narrow integrations**. *The difference from the other three is not depth or price — it is that MagicDoor is the **only** competitor whose positioning sentence and PropertyAI's positioning sentence are the same sentence.*

### What the matrix actually says

- **PropertyAI leads on exactly three rows:** AI maintenance triage (#4), AI chatbot/voice (#18), and — from the ground truth, not tabled here to keep this matrix to market-relevant rows — predictive maintenance/IoT, white-label, emergency routing, workflow automation and contractor-mobile. **Depth of AI ops is real and is the one honest moat.**
- **PropertyAI trails or ties on twelve rows it must fix to be credible at the low end:** #5 screening (stub), #6 AI scoring (mock), #11 owner statements, #13 trust accounting, #14 1099 e-filing, #15 deposits, #17 bulk SMS, #19 self-serve onboarding, #20 data import, #21 published pricing, #22 free tier/trial, #23 QuickBooks (stub).
- **Note the shape of the gaps:** most of them are **packaging and plumbing, not research**. Trust accounting, 1099s and owner statements are *accounting features*; bulk SMS and self-serve import are *one-to-two-sprint items*; published pricing and a free tier are *commercial decisions*. Only real screening (#5/#6) plus its compliance guardrail is genuinely hard — and it is also the one with real legal exposure.

**Blunt summary of C:** PropertyAI has built a **better product than four competitors on one axis (AI-native ops depth)** and is **unable to sell it to anyone** in the tier it claims as its funnel, because it has no free tier, no self-serve checkout, no published price, no import path and no fast onboarding. On top of that, to actually *serve* a T2 firm it is missing **trust accounting, owner statements/distributions, 1099 e-filing, real screening and bank reconciliation** — the exact five things Rentec PM at $65/mo and MagicDoor at $2.50/active-lease already ship.

## D. Strategic read

### (i) Why MagicDoor is the dangerous one — and the exact price maths

MagicDoor is dangerous **not because it is cheaper at PropertyAI's target scale, but because it is cheaper at the scale where PropertyAI's funnel is supposed to start.** Run the numbers honestly:

| Portfolio | MagicDoor | PropertyAI (proposed pricing, positioning doc — *inference*) | Read |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–3 units | **$2.50–$7.50/mo** (or $20/mo flat for up to 10 leases) | $79/mo Starter floor | **MagicDoor is 10–30× cheaper.** PropertyAI is not even in the consideration set. |
| 10 units | **$20/mo** (Starter, up to 10 leases) | $79/mo | **~4× cheaper.** |
| 80 units | **~$200/mo** ($2.50 × 80 active leases) | Growth ~$160–200/mo ($2.0–2.5/unit; $149 floor) | **At parity.** PropertyAI cannot win here on price. |
| 200 units | **~$500/mo** | Growth ~$400–500/mo | **At parity or slightly above.** |

Two conclusions follow, and they are the important ones:

1. **At 80–300 units the price war does not exist** — PropertyAI's and MagicDoor's unit economics converge. So the "MagicDoor is 10× cheaper" fear is really a **funnel** fear: MagicDoor (and Hemlane's free tier) own the landlord from unit 1 to unit 50, and by the time PropertyAI's Growth tier becomes economic, the customer already has a working AI-native platform they are paying $20–$200/mo for. **PropertyAI loses the customer before the pricing maths ever favours it.**
2. **MagicDoor's "per active lease" model is a structural advantage PropertyAI should study, not copy blindly.** Billing occupancy rather than doors means vacancy months are free — which is exactly the wrong economics for a vendor but exactly the right message for a landlord with frictional vacancy. It also removes the "I'm paying for units I can't rent" objection that a per-unit floor creates.

**What PropertyAI must NOT try to beat MagicDoor on:**
- **Price per unit at the low end.** A $2.50/lease competitor cannot be undercut by an AI-heavy platform without destroying gross margin. Concede the 1–10 unit price point; capture it with a *free* tier instead.
- **"AI included, no tiers, no add-ons" simplicity.** MagicDoor already owns the cleanest version of that message. PropertyAI's complexity is justified only by depth (trust accounting, portfolio ops, compliance) — so **do not market simplicity**.
- **Speed-to-first-listing for a solo landlord.** MagicDoor set up in 30 minutes with SMS-only tenants is a better product for that person.
- **A permanent free-forever tier.** Match Hemlane/MagicDoor on *entry*, not on *residency*: use a capped free tier as a funnel, never as a home.

**Where PropertyAI can legitimately beat MagicDoor:** trust/portfolio accounting depth at scale, real integrations and an open API, compliance-grade screening, IoT/predictive maintenance, white-label, and enterprise-ish ops (multi-entity, audit, escalation). All of those are **T2/T3 arguments**, which is precisely consistent with the existing positioning decision.

### (ii) The funnel problem — where do the first 100 customers actually come from?

State the problem plainly: **with no free tier, no self-serve checkout, no published pricing and no import path, PropertyAI has no product-led funnel.** Therefore its first 100 customers cannot come from the tiers it says it will harvest — they must come from a human-mediated motion with a higher floor. That is not fatal, but it changes the plan:

| Realistic path | How it works | Honest assessment |
|---|---|---|
| **Founder-led / inside sales to owner-operators of 80–300 units** (the wedge: "AI takes maintenance off your phone") | List-build → demo the CV triage → 14-day pilot | The **only path that works today**. Needs ~$500–2,000 CAC against a ~$1,800–3,000 ACV → a 25–60% CAC ratio, viable *only* with high retention + expansion. Requires a sales hire before revenue. |
| **Channel: NARPM chapters, landlord associations, REIA meetups, PM bookkeepers/CPAs** | Referral + co-selling; a trusted accountant introducing a bookkeeping-adjacent ops layer | Cheap and high-trust, but slow and geographically clustered. |
| **Wedge-alongside, not replacement** | Sell maintenance+comms as a layer *on top of* Buildium/AppFolio | Requires their APIs (limited/gated) → friction. Also requires the *hook* to be valuable without trust accounting. |
| **White-label / OEM** | License the ops layer to a small PM franchise or a regional PM group | **Under-used asset that no competitor has** — arguably the fastest credible route to 100 accounts without a T1 funnel. |
| **Free utility as top-of-funnel** | A free AI maintenance-triage / rent-estimate tool for 1 property | The cheapest *substitute* for a free tier. |
| **Migration-led offer** | "We'll move your Buildium/AppFolio data in X days" | Works, but only after C1 #8 (import) is built. |

**Bottom line:** the pricing shape in the positioning doc (self-serve, transparent, per-unit with a floor) **assumes a funnel that does not exist yet.** Either build the funnel (free tier + checkout + published price + import) or drop the self-serve pretence and fund a services-heavy, founder-led motion with a higher monthly floor. Doing neither is the worst option — and it is the current state.

### (iii) 3–5 concrete, cheap fixes to be credible at the low end *without* abandoning T2

Ordered by cost-to-impact. Fixes 1–4 are **packaging/plumbing, not research**; fix 5 is the expensive one that is nonetheless non-negotiable.

1. **Ship a capped free tier ("PropertyAI Free") for 1–2 units / up to 3 leases.** Rent collection + maintenance triage + tenant portal + SMS, no card, self-serve. It is *packaging over modules that already exist*. Monetise transaction fees (exactly Hemlane/TurboTenant's model). Hard caps, no support SLA, no free-forever at 5+ units → **a funnel, not a home.** This is the single highest-leverage change.
2. **Publish the price and turn on credit-card self-checkout — literally a pricing-page and billing-integration change, not a product change.** Publish per-unit pricing with the floor and a **live unit-count slider** (Rentec's price-transparency play is the most-praised practice in this whole set). You cannot be "the transparent alternative to AppFolio" while being more opaque than TenantCloud.
3. **Ship self-serve data import: CSV + a guided "switch from Buildium / AppFolio / TenantCloud" mapper, plus a "trial with your own data" mode where the data survives conversion** (Rentec's model — its most under-copied feature). Zero import code exists today; this is the cheapest way to make switching *easier than staying*.
4. **Ship bulk/automated SMS + automated late-fee comms to tenants.** Zero `bulkSms` code today, table stakes at both T1 and T2, and it converts the comms story into the channel tenants reply to. Bundle it into every tier — do not gate it.
5. **Fund the five things a small PM firm will otherwise refuse to move for: trust accounting, owner statements + distributions, 1099 e-filing, bank feeds/reconciliation, and real screening behind a fair-housing/FCRA guardrail.** This is the one *expensive* fix, and it is the difference between "an AI layer that sits next to Buildium" and "a platform that can take the book of business." Do it **after** 1–4 (which create the funnel that will amortise it) — but do not pretend items 1–4 make PropertyAI T2-credible on their own.

**What not to do:** don't chase MagicDoor's $2.50 entry price; don't ship a permanent free-forever tier; don't market "simpler" (that's MagicDoor's and Hemlane's message); don't build the CV/voice depth further out ahead of the boring accounting/onboarding/plumbing gaps — the depth is already ahead of the ability to sell it.
